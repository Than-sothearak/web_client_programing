Detailed Information About Cambodia

Historical Highlights

Cambodia was once the center of the powerful Khmer Empire, which ruled much of Southeast Asia between the 9th and 15th centuries. The empire built incredible structures like Angkor Wat and Angkor Thom. These historical sites remain important symbols of national pride today.

Famous Places in Cambodia

  1. Angkor Wat The largest religious monument in the world
  2. Phnom Penh The capital city with royal palaces and museums
  3. Siem Reap A tourist hub near the Angkor temple complex

Culture and Traditions

  • Buddhism is the main religion practiced by most people
  • Traditional dance such as Apsara represents ancient stories
  • Khmer New Year is one of the biggest festivals
  • Family and community values are very important

Economy and Development

Cambodia’s economy is growing quickly, especially in sectors like tourism, construction, agriculture, and technology. Many young people are learning digital skills such as web development, design, and entrepreneurship.

Popular Cambodian Foods

  • Amok A traditional coconut curry dish
  • Lok Lak Stir-fried beef with sauce
  • Nom Banh Chok Khmer noodles
  • Fresh tropical fruits like mango, durian, and coconut
