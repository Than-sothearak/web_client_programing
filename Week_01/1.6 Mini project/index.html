Welcome to Cambodia

Cambodia, officially known as the Kingdom of Cambodia, is a country located in mainland Southeast Asia. It shares borders with Thailand to the northwest, Laos to the north, and Vietnam to the east, and it also has a coastline along the Gulf of Thailand in the southwest. The country covers an area of about 181,000 square kilometers and is mostly made up of low plains, with important natural features such as the Mekong River and Tonlé Sap Lake, the largest lake in Southeast Asia. Cambodia has a tropical climate and a population of around 17 million people, most of whom are Khmer. The capital city is Phnom Penh, followed by major cities like Siem Reap and Battambang.

Cambodia has a long and rich history. In the year 802 AD, King Jayavarman II declared himself the ruler and united the Khmer regions, marking the beginning of the powerful Khmer Empire. This empire played an important role in spreading both Hinduism and Buddhism across Southeast Asia. During this time, many impressive temples and structures were built, including the famous Angkor Wat. However, the empire began to decline in the 15th century. Later, in 1863, Cambodia became a protectorate of France. After being occupied by Japan during World War II, Cambodia gained independence from France in 1953.

The culture of Cambodia is deeply influenced by Buddhism, which plays an important role in daily life. Traditional arts such as Apsara dance, silk weaving, and sculpture reflect the country’s identity. Cambodian cuisine is also unique, featuring dishes like Amok, Lok Lak, and fresh tropical fruits.

Today, Cambodia is a country full of opportunities and natural beauty. From rivers and forests to modern cities and ancient temples, it offers a perfect mix of tradition and progress. Whether you are a traveler, student, or investor, Cambodia has something valuable to offer.
